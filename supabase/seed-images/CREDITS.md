# Seed image credits

Demo photos and videos for the fictional seed orgs, all openly licensed from Wikimedia Commons. They show volunteers at work, animals and places, not the people being served. Org logos (`*/logo.png`) are original marks for these fictional orgs, drawn as SVG with wordmarks set in Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License) by `scripts/gen-logos.mjs`.

# 0004 Phase I scope cuts
**Status:** accepted, 2026-09-30

**In:** claimed orgs only, donor accounts + follows + simple profile, org posts + YouTube/Vimeo/Instagram embeds, chronological feed + cause filter, Every.org donate, light AI assists.
**Out (later):** individual fundraisers, IRS stub profiles, comments, personalized ranking, native video hosting, per-gift impact, email delivery.
**Why:** individual fundraisers bring fraud, medical privacy and payment liability. Comments need moderation. Ranking conflicts with principle #4.

# 0012 Grants are public only once the nonprofit confirms them
**Status:** accepted, 2026-10-13

Foundations record grants (and can tag grantees on posts). A grant becomes public only after the grantee's staff confirm it; until then only the two orgs' staff see it. Editing a confirmed grant's substance un-confirms it. Profile totals ("$X to N nonprofits") are computed from confirmed grants.

**Why:** principle #1 (no fabrication). A foundation can't claim credit for funding it didn't give, and a nonprofit can't be associated with a funder it doesn't recognize.
**Trade-off:** grants look "missing" until confirmed; the dashboard prompt ("Grants to confirm") keeps that short.

# 0019 Public figures join and share; we don't compile pages about them
**Status:** accepted, 2026-10-16.

People can follow public figures and see who they give to (F-113). A public figure is someone who joined imnet themselves and was verified by an admin (`profiles.public_figure`, not settable by users). What they show is their own choice: giving appears only if they opt in (`show_giving_totals`), like anyone else.

**Why not pages about famous people built from news or filings:** it would put words in real people's mouths (impersonation), risk wrong attributions (no fabrication), and claim consent nobody gave. Public filings of a person's own foundation remain viewable through NomBot (F-110) on the foundation's page.
**Demo:** the public figures in the demo are fictional and labeled as demo data.
**Next:** a "request verification" flow for public figures (today admins set the flag), and optional links from a public figure to their own foundation's profile.

# 0010 Model the structure on LinkedIn, keep imnet's identity and principles
**Status:** accepted, 2026-10-13. Supersedes the "Feed" line of 0009.

**Copied from LinkedIn:** icon nav with search, three-column home (profile card, feed, suggestions), boxed post cards with an action bar and "see more", org pages with banner, overlapping logo and Home/About/Posts tabs, donor profiles with banner and section cards.

**Not copied:**
- Public like or reaction counts (principle #4). "Moved me" stays private.
- Comments (no moderation yet).
- Engagement-ranked feed. Feeds stay newest first; suggestions are alphabetical.
- LinkedIn's blue and branding. imnet keeps its own type and colors (0009).

**Why:** donors and nonprofit staff already know how LinkedIn works, so the network feels familiar on day one.

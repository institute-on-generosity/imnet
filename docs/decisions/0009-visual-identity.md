# 0009 shadcn for function, a custom brand layer for identity
**Status:** accepted, 2026-10-13

shadcn/ui (Base UI) for functional UI: forms, dashboard, admin, dialogs. A brand layer on top, set as tokens in `app/globals.css`:
- **Type:** Literata (stories, headlines), Schibsted Grotesk (interface).
- **Color:** paper `#f5f6f4`, ink `#14213d`, leaf `#1f7a5a` (sources, verified), marigold `#f4b400` ("moved", asks).
- **Signature:** impact stats as a footnoted ledger. Every figure links to its numbered source, because traceability is the product's promise.
- **Feed:** ~~stories are not boxed cards~~ superseded by 0010 (LinkedIn-style boxed cards). Serif story text stays.

**Why:** out of the box shadcn looks like every SaaS dashboard; imnet is a consumer, story-first product.
